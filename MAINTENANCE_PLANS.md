### Email infrastructure

- **Transactional email delivery.** Every submission from your contact form is delivered straight to your inbox through a dedicated sender (`leads@updates.spraybuzzoff.com`) with bot and spam protection already in place.
- **50,000 emails per month included.** Enough headroom to run real campaigns (mosquito-season launch, fall tune-ups, holiday promos, follow-ups) without ever touching a Mailchimp or Constant Contact bill. Sent through the same Resend infrastructure the contact form uses.
- **Spam protection and domain reputation.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records on your sender domain are monitored and kept current so emails land in the inbox, not the promotions tab or spam folder.
- **Lead-form bot filtering.** Incoming contact submissions are scored for spam (disposable email domains, cold-pitch phrases, non-local phone numbers, etc.). Real leads land in your main inbox; suspicious ones are quarantined separately so they never clutter your pipeline.
- **Bounce and unsubscribe handling.** Hard bounces are removed from your list automatically so you don't accumulate dead addresses that hurt deliverability. Unsubscribe links are built into every template for CAN-SPAM compliance.
- **Branded email templates.** Up to 2 custom-designed templates per month in your green-and-gold botanical palette (monthly newsletter, seasonal promo, booking confirmation, post-service thank-you, review request, etc.). You send me the copy; I build the template and schedule the send.

### Local search & visibility

- **Google Business Profile upkeep.** I'll publish 2 to 4 updates per month to your GBP (seasonal posts, photos, service highlights, customer wins). Active listings rank higher on "pest control near me" searches, and most of your competitors don't do this.
- **Business-listing audit.** Every month I'll check that your business name, address, and phone number are spelled consistently across Google, Yelp, Facebook, Nextdoor, Apple Maps, Bing, Angi, and the BBB. Google penalizes inconsistent listings, and they drift over time as directories auto-update from scraped data.
- **Monthly Search Console report.** I'll pull your traffic data, write a short summary (what's ranking, what's slipping, which keywords are growing), and send it to you by the 5th of each month.
- **Keyword tracking.** I watch your position on the searches that actually bring in calls: "pest control Rancho Cucamonga," "mosquito control Upland," "natural pest control Inland Empire," "exterminator near me," and so on, so we know what's working.
- **City landing page upkeep.** Your 8 city pages (Rancho Cucamonga, Upland, Ontario, Claremont, Glendora, San Dimas, Fontana, Pomona) get content refreshes, new local testimonials, and new neighborhood mentions each quarter.
- **On-page tune-ups.** Titles, descriptions, and page structure adjusted based on what's actually converting.
- **AI search optimization.** I'll keep your site's `llms.txt` file current so when someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity "who does plant-based pest control in the Inland Empire," Buzz Off is one of the answers.
- **Structured data upkeep.** The behind-the-scenes code that tells Google about your services, service area, hours, and reviews. It gets added automatically when I add pages or services, so it's always current.
- **Review snippet maintenance.** New 5-star Google and Yelp reviews are pulled into the homepage's structured data each month so the "★★★★★ 5.0" rich result keeps reflecting reality.
