# Buzz Off Pest Prevention (spraybuzzoff.com)

Client site for **Buzz Off Pest Prevention**, a plant-based pest control company in
Rancho Cucamonga, CA, serving the Inland Empire, San Gabriel Valley, and parts of
Orange County. Built and maintained by **Plain Talk Developers** (Ignacio Nunez /
John 155 LLC).

**Hayley and Veronnica** founded it, two moms who wanted an alternative to synthetic
pesticides. **Dorrin** is the day to day client contact and is not technical, so
anything written for him, emails especially, goes in plain business English with no
jargon. **Maurice ("Moe")** is the technician customers name most often in reviews.

Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind v4, framer-motion, TypeScript, on Vercel.
Static marketing site: the only server route is `/api/lead`.

## Conventions

- **No em dashes in user-facing copy.** Commas, periods, colons, or "·". Applies to
  UI strings, emails, docs, and commit messages. `scripts/strip-emdash.js` sweeps
  the tree if some slip in.
- **Brand tokens live in `src/app/globals.css` under `@theme {}`.** `BRAND.md` is the
  written guide and points at them. Do not invent hex values, use the tokens.
- `core.autocrlf` is **true**, so the working tree is CRLF and git stores LF. Multi-line
  string replacements in scripts must normalize `\r\n` to `\n` first or they will
  silently fail to match.
- Dev server runs on **port 3007** (`.claude/launch.json`, `start-dev.cmd`).

## Reviews pipeline

This is the part most likely to be broken by someone not knowing the rules.

`src/lib/reviews.ts` is the **single source of truth** for every review shown on the
site. It merges two files:

| File | Origin | Edit by hand? |
|---|---|---|
| `src/data/google-reviews.json` | Generated by `npm run fetch-reviews` | **Never.** It is overwritten. |
| `src/data/manual-reviews.json` | Hand-maintained | Yes. Yelp reviews, plus Google ones no longer on the listing. |

Rules that are not obvious from reading the code:

- **Never hardcode a review count, rating, or "N+ reviews" string anywhere.** Import
  `TOTAL_REVIEW_COUNT`, `AVERAGE_RATING`, `FIVE_STAR_PERCENT`, or `REVIEWS` instead.
  Hardcoded counts went stale twice and shipped wrong numbers to production, once in
  the JSON-LD `aggregateRating` and once in the `/reviews` page metadata.
- **`sort_by` must stay `newestFirst`.** SerpApi's `ratingHigh` stops paginating early
  without erroring. It returned 24 of 40 reviews here and 129 of 154 on Standout
  Exterior, silently, for months.
- **Review images are downloaded into `public/assets/reviews/`, never hotlinked.**
  Google revokes its `lh3.googleusercontent.com` review-image tokens after a few days,
  so any committed Google URL becomes a broken image in production.
- **The fetch prunes orphans.** Google rotates the token inside its photo URLs, so the
  same picture hashes to a new filename on a later run. Without pruning, the repo gains
  a full photo set every month and never loses one.
- Reviews render an **absolute month** derived from `isoDate`. Google's relative dates
  ("5 months ago") freeze at fetch time and drift wrong.
- Two Google entries are star ratings with no text. They count toward the total but are
  filtered out of the grid, which is why the page says "51 of our 53".

`SERPAPI_KEY` lives in `.env.local` and in repo secrets.
`.github/workflows/review-refresh-monthly.yml` re-runs the fetch on the 1st, commits the
result, and emails a summary only when something changed.

## Lead pipeline

`/api/lead` posts into the client's **GorillaDesk** CRM (`src/lib/gorilladesk.ts`).

- If the CRM write fails, `src/lib/lead-alert.ts` emails the lead to ops through Resend
  so it is never lost. Do not remove that fallback.
- Rate limited to 3 requests per minute per IP, in memory.
- A request carrying a matching `x-health-check` header runs validation and a read-only
  CRM call, then returns 200 **without creating a customer**. That is how the weekly
  monitor proves the pipeline works without depositing junk in the client's CRM every
  Monday. The header only works when `HEALTH_CHECK_SECRET` is set on the deployment.

## SEO automation stack

Eight scheduled workflows in `.github/workflows/`, with their scripts in
`.github/scripts/phase2/`. `seo-stack.config.mjs` is the per-site config and holds the
site URL, the Search Console property, email from/to, and the NAP record. It contains
no secrets and is safe to commit.

Weekly on Mondays: health check, keyword tracker, Lighthouse, SEO opportunities.
Monthly on the 1st: NAP audit, SEO digest, review refresh.

Required repo secrets: `SERPAPI_KEY`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, `HEALTH_CHECK_SECRET`,
`GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_SECRET`, `GOOGLE_OAUTH_REFRESH_TOKEN`.

Search Console is verified as a **domain** property, so the identifier is the
`sc-domain:` form, not a URL prefix.

## Testing and CI

**No workflow runs lint or build.** Vercel builds on push, and that is the only gate.
`npm run build` and `npx tsc --noEmit` before pushing anything non-trivial.

`npm run lint` reports pre-existing errors in `about-us`, `contact`, `not-found`,
`pest-control/[city]`, `services`, `Nav.tsx`, and the CommonJS scripts in `scripts/`.
Those predate the current work. Do not treat a clean lint run as the bar, but do not
add new violations either.

## Known debt

- `TESTIMONIALS` in `src/lib/constants.ts` is dead code. Nothing imports it, and it
  holds a third conflicting copy of review text that already exists in the two data
  files. It should be deleted.
- **Some testimonials look embellished.** Several entries tagged Yelp are expanded
  rewrites of real Google reviews. The Terrance review is 4 sentences on Google and 8
  on the site, with added specifics. "Julie S." existed in three inconsistent versions.
  Four reviews labeled Google are not on the listing at all. Flagged to the owner and
  not resolved. **Do not add testimonial text that is not verifiably from a real
  review**, and prefer the fetched corpus over anything hand-typed.
